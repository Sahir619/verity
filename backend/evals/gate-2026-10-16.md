## Spend and probes

- Real runs on disk: 28 (10 gate, 18 follow-up), summed from `final/llm-calls.json`: **3.0522 USD**. Reasoning probe: about 0.005 USD (14 calls under 250 tokens). Total about 3.06 USD of the 5.00 USD budget.
- Reasoning probe (Super and Ultra, one tiny prompt each): `completion_tokens` includes `reasoning_tokens`. `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=false` gave 0 reasoning on both models. Super honoured `reasoning_effort` none (0) and low (150 down to 9) and `low_effort` (150 down to 8). Ultra `reasoning_effort=low` equalled its default (49 vs 49); `medium_effort` reached the template (prompt +7 tokens) but did not lower reasoning on that prompt. One sample per setting, so indicative only. None is enabled.
- Truncation: at the old 12,000 cap, 11 of about 20 matmul4 rtl_gen calls, 6 repair calls and 1 optimizer call ended with `finish_reason: length`, always with all 12,000 tokens spent on reasoning. Fix applied: `rtl_max_output` 24,000, `llm_super_s` 120, and `plan_max_output` 12,000 (the planner hit 6,000 twice on matmul4).
- After the cap raise, matmul4 baseline passed 2 of 3 (rtl cap 24,000) and then 1 of 3 (plus plan cap 12,000). The failures are not truncations any more: all three samples failed the contract/lint stage with an inferred latch (`always_comb` signal without a default assignment) or a declaration inside a procedural block, and two repairs did not clear it. Counting all matmul4 runs, 6 of 9 passed (3 of 3 before the raise, which survived on retries and repairs).

## Sandbox measurement of reference RTL with power, 2026-10-16 (no LLM, zero spend)
