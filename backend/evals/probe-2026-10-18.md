# Range probe, 2026-10-18

Goal: ship only knob ranges whose edges pass on the real model, and cut settings that are too slow or prove nothing for a judge.

## Setup

- Real model: Nebius Token Factory, Nemotron 3 Ultra for plan, RTL and repair (provider confirmed `nebius` through `GET /health` before every batch).
- Real tools: `verity-tools:0.1` Docker image (Icarus + cocotb, Yosys, OpenSTA, EQY), Nangate45.
- Fast mode (one version, no evolution), priority area, through the local API exactly as the bench calls it (`POST /runs` with `params`, SSE to the terminal event).
- Every run also measures the pack's human-written `reference.v` at the same knobs and runs EQY reference vs Verity's winner (`human_ref` event).
- Spend cap $2.50 for the probe, checked before each run. Spent: **$0.69 over 17 runs** (scripts and raw JSONL kept outside the repo).

## What it shows

- Every add8, cnt8 and fsm_seq edge passes its locked tests on the first version and is proven equivalent to the human design.
- mac4 passes its locked tests everywhere it synthesizes. The equivalence proof is the limit: at 8 lanes, inputs wider than 8 bits come back inconclusive after about 100 to 125 s of EQY, and one 6 x 16-bit run did not synthesize within the tool limit. Up to 8 lanes x 8 bits (and 4 lanes x 12 bits) the proof completes in under a minute.
- Fast-mode wall time is under 2 minutes on every proven setting.
- Two real "beat the human" results at the fsm_seq edges: Verity's first version is 62% smaller than the human reference for pattern 111 without overlap (19.68 vs 51.87 µm²) and 38% smaller for 10110011 with overlap (48.94 vs 78.74 µm²), both proven equivalent. These are single fast-mode runs, not averages.

## Decision (shipped ranges)

| Family | Knob | Range |
|---|---|---|
| mac4 | lanes | 1 to 8 |
| mac4 | width | **4 to 8** (was 4 to 16) |
| mac4 | cycles | 4 to 32 |
| add8 | width | 4 to 32 |
| cnt8 | width | 4 to 16 (cut from 32 earlier so every width runs a full wrap test) |
| cnt8 | enable | on or off |
| fsm_seq | pattern | 3 to 8 bits |
| fsm_seq | overlap | on or off |

Every corner of the shipped mac4 box (1x4x4, 8x8x32, and the default 4x8x16 from the 2026-10-16 gate) is proven on the real model. 4 lanes x 12 bits is also proven, but a per-knob range cannot express it without letting in 8 x 12, so width stops at 8.
